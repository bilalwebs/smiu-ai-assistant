# SMIU Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Q: What is SMIU?
Sir Syed Management Sciences University (SMIU) is a private university in Karachi, Pakistan, offering programs in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Business Administration.

### Q: Is SMIU recognized by HEC?
Yes, SMIU is fully recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan and is ranked among the top private universities in Sindh.

### Q: What is the medium of instruction?
English is the primary medium of instruction for all programs.

## Admission

### Q: Can I apply without an entry test score?
No, the SMIU Aptitude Test is mandatory for all undergraduate programs. However, SAT scores (minimum 1100) may be accepted as an equivalent.

### Q: Is there a waitlist for admissions?
Yes, if seats are filled, eligible candidates are placed on a waitlist and offered admission if seats become available.

### Q: Can I defer my admission?
Yes, you can defer admission for up to one semester by submitting a formal request to the Registrar's office before the semester begins.

## Academic

### Q: What is the semester system?
SMIU follows a semester system with two regular semesters (Fall and Spring) and one optional summer semester.

### Q: What is the credit hour requirement for BS programs?
BS programs require a minimum of 130-140 credit hours for graduation, depending on the specific program.

### Q: How many courses per semester?
Students typically take 5-6 courses per semester, totaling 15-18 credit hours.

### Q: Is there a GPA requirement to remain enrolled?
Yes, students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.0. Students falling below this threshold will receive academic probation.

### Q: Can I change my major after admission?
Yes, you can request a major change in the first two semesters by submitting a change request to the academic advisor.

## Facilities

### Q: Does SMIU provide hostel accommodation?
Yes, SMIU offers separate hostel facilities for male and female students. Rooms are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Q: Is there a library on campus?
Yes, SMIU has a fully equipped library with digital resources, e-books, and access to IEEE and ACM databases.

### Q: Does SMIU have a job placement cell?
Yes, the Career Services Office assists students with internships, job placements, and career counseling.

## Financial

### Q: Does SMIU offer installment plans?
Yes, tuition fees can be paid in two installments per semester with a small administrative fee.

### Q: Are there any hidden charges?
No, all fees are clearly listed in the fee structure. The only additional costs may be exam re-sit fees and transcript fees.

### Q: How do I apply for financial aid?
Submit a financial aid application with supporting documents (income certificate, family CNIC) to the Financial Aid Office before the deadline.
