# SMIU Admissions Guide 2025-2026

## Overview

Sir Syed Management Sciences University (SMIU) offers undergraduate and graduate programs in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Business Administration.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Undergraduate Programs (BS)
- **Minimum qualification:** Intermediate (HSC) or equivalent with at least 50% marks
- **Required subjects:** Mathematics, Physics (for CS/AI/DS programs)
- **Age limit:** Maximum 25 years at the time of admission

### Graduate Programs (MS/MPhil)
- **Minimum qualification:** 16 years of education with at least 50% marks or CGPA 2.5/4.0
- **Relevant bachelor's degree** in the chosen field of study

## Admission Process

1. **Online Application:** Submit application through the SMIU admissions portal at admissions.smiu.edu.pk
2. **Document Submission:** Upload scanned copies of:
   - Matric/O-Level certificate
   - Intermediate/A-Level certificate
   - CNIC/B-Form
   - Passport-size photographs
   - Domicile certificate
3. **Entry Test:** Appear in the SMIU Aptitude Test (SAT/ECAT equivalency accepted)
4. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates will be called for a panel interview
5. **Merit List:** Final merit calculated as 50% academic + 30% test + 20% interview

## Fee Structure

### BS Computer Science (Per Semester)
- Tuition Fee: PKR 175,000
- Admission Fee (one-time): PKR 25,000
- Security Deposit (refundable): PKR 10,000
- Total First Semester: PKR 210,000

### BS Data Science / AI (Per Semester)
- Tuition Fee: PKR 185,000
- Admission Fee (one-time): PKR 25,000
- Security Deposit (refundable): PKR 10,000
- Total First Semester: PKR 220,000

## Scholarships

- **Merit Scholarship:** Top 10% of each batch receive 25% tuition waiver
- **Need-Based Financial Aid:** Up to 50% tuition waiver for eligible students
- **Sibling Discount:** 10% waiver if sibling is currently enrolled
- **Sports Quota:** Up to 15% waiver for national-level athletes
