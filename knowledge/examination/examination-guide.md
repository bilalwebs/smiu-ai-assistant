# SMIU Examination Guidelines

## Examination System

SMIU follows a continuous evaluation system with the following components:

### Grading Components
| Component | Weightage |
|-----------|-----------|
| Mid-Term Exam | 25% |
| Final Exam | 35% |
| Assignments | 15% |
| Quizzes | 10% |
| Class Participation | 5% |
| Project/Final Report | 10% |

## Important Rules

### Examination Schedule
- Mid-term exams are held in the 8th week of each semester
- Final exams are held after the 16th week
- Makeup exams must be requested at least 5 days before the exam date

### Attendance Policy
- Minimum 75% attendance required in each course
- Students with less than 75% attendance will receive a grade penalty
- Medical leave requires a valid medical certificate submitted within 3 days

### Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism in any form will result in automatic failure of the course
- Cheating during exams will result in a zero on the exam and potential suspension
- All assignments must be original work with proper citations

## Re-sit Examination

### Eligibility
- Students who fail a course (grade below C-) may apply for a re-sit exam
- Re-sit exams are held in the next semester's exam period
- Maximum one re-sit attempt per course

### Fee
- Re-sit exam fee: PKR 3,000 per course
- Must be paid before the re-sit exam date

## Transcript Request

### Official Transcript
- Processed within 5-7 working days
- Fee: PKR 1,000 per copy
- Can be requested through the Registrar's office or online portal

### Unofficial Transcript
- Available immediately through the student portal
- Free of charge
